Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about searching, comparing, and booking flights.

Where do flight prices and options come from?

Searches retrieve current flight options from our flight search provider. Availability and prices can change before you book.

How are flights ranked?

Choose Cheapest, Best Value, or Premium. Best Value weighs fare, travel time, and stops; you can change the ranking from your results.

Where do I complete my booking?

Select a flight to continue to the booking provider. The provider handles availability, passenger details, and payment.
